Pricing Questions

Is the Free plan really free forever?

Yes. There is no time limit, no card required, and it is not a trial. The one limit is that you can add 20 new patients per month; existing patients and their records are never capped or locked. Upgrade only if you outgrow it.

What's the difference between monthly and annual?

Annual billing saves you about 17% versus paying month to month. Starter is ₹399/mo billed monthly, or ₹3,990 for the year, a saving of ₹798. Pro is ₹799/mo billed monthly, or ₹7,990 for the year, a saving of ₹1,598.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, credit/debit cards and net banking through our PCI-compliant gateway. You can also collect patient payments the same way inside Dentomate.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Anytime. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your account. Your data stays intact and exportable.

Do you charge per patient?

No. Paid plans include unlimited patients. You never pay more as your practice grows.

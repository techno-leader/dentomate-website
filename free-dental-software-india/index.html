Common Questions

Is Dentomate's free dental software really free?

Yes. The Free plan costs ₹0 and doesn't expire. It isn't a 14-day or 30-day trial, you don't enter a card to start, and nothing stops working after a set number of days.

What's the catch with the free plan?

One limit, stated plainly: you can register 20 new patients per calendar month. Existing patients, their visits, prescriptions and bills are unlimited. WhatsApp recall campaigns and custom letterhead are not on the free plan.

What happens when I hit 20 new patients in a month?

New patient registration is blocked until the next calendar month, or until you upgrade. Your existing records stay fully accessible, and you can keep recording visits, prescriptions and payments for patients already in the system.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. Sign up with an email address or your Google account and start adding patients immediately. No card details are collected on the Free plan.

Is my patient data safe on the free plan?

Yes. Free accounts get the same protection as paid ones: encrypted storage, data hosted in India, and handling aligned to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. See the security page for details.

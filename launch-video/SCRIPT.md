# Dentomate — Product Launch Video

**Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264 MP4 + square (1080×1080) and vertical (1080×1920) social cuts
**Runtime:** 61.4 seconds
**Style:** Motion-graphics SaaS launch film — kinetic opener, real product UI in device frames, counter animations.

Audio is specified separately in `SOUND-DESIGN.md`, including the voiceover
script and a timecoded cue sheet. The score currently in the repo is a
placeholder.

## Sources of truth

Every claim, number and screenshot is taken from Dentomate's own material — the
product UI captures in `assets/images/` (real `app.dentomate.in` screens) and
the copy on the marketing site. No stock photography, no invented metrics.

## Text budget

The rule this cut follows: **no scene asks the viewer to read more than one
headline plus two short lines.** Paragraphs, feature lists and sub-headlines
were removed — a showreel is watched, not read, and most of it plays muted in a
feed at half size. Where a fact matters it is a number, not a sentence.

Scenes 0 and 7 carry almost no text at all, by design: they are the two moments
the film asks for attention rather than comprehension.

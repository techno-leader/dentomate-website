# Dentomate — Product Launch Video

**Format:** 1920×1080, 60 fps, H.264 MP4 + square (1080×1080) and vertical (1080×1920) social cuts
**Runtime:** 72.8 seconds
**Style:** Motion-graphics SaaS launch film — kinetic type, real product UI in device frames, counter animations, synced soundtrack.

## Sources of truth

Every claim, number and screenshot in this video is taken from Dentomate's own
material — the product UI captures in `assets/images/` (real `app.dentomate.in`
screens: Dashboard, WhatsApp Inbox, Patient Details, Analytics, Clinic switcher)
and the copy on the marketing site. No stock photography, no invented metrics.

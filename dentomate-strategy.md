# Dentomate — Marketing Positioning Strategy

## Chosen Position: "Kill the WhatsApp Tax"
Lead with the cost shock. Dentists paying ₹6,000–15,000/month for WhatsApp API + developer maintenance will feel this immediately. Dentomate replaces all of it, purpose-built for dental, with zero ongoing API costs.

---

## Core Positioning Statement
"Dentomate is the practice growth platform built exclusively for Indian dental clinics. It replaces costly WhatsApp setups and manual follow-ups with an automated, branded patient communication system that sends prescriptions, organises every record, and brings patients back — month after month."

---

## The Three Problems We Solve

**1. The WhatsApp Tax**
Indian dentists are paying ₹6,000–15,000/month for WhatsApp Business API plus a developer to maintain it. The result: unbranded messages, underused features, and a monthly bill that doesn't grow their practice.

**2. The Follow-up Gap**
Patients leave the chair and forget. No branded prescription to look at, no follow-up reminder, no reason to return in 6 months. The clinic that sends a beautiful branded prescription 5 minutes after the patient leaves is the one they remember and return to.

**3. The Record Chaos**
Patient history scattered across WhatsApp, paper files, and spreadsheets. No visibility into who's overdue for a visit, which treatment generates the most revenue, or how each clinic branch is performing.

---

## Target Personas

### Persona 1 — Solo / Small Clinic Dentist
- 1–3 chairs, 20–60 patients/week
- Currently using WhatsApp Business manually or via expensive API
- Pain: High cost + low ROI from current patient comms
- Motivation: More returning patients without more manual work
- Decision driver: Cost savings + automation

### Persona 2 — Multi-Clinic Owner
- 3–15 locations, team of 5–50 staff
- Pain: No central visibility, staff using personal phones for patient comms, no branding consistency
- Motivation: Unified control, branded communications across all locations, consolidated analytics
- Decision driver: Control + scalability

## Tone & Voice Guidelines

- **Direct, not clever** — State the cost, state the outcome. Dentists are busy.
- **Numbers first** — Indian B2B buyers trust specific data over vague claims.
- **India-native** — Use ₹ (never $). Reference WhatsApp naturally. Use Indian clinic context.
- **Confident, not arrogant** — "Pilot clinics saw 30% more returning patients" not "We're India's #1 dental software."
- **Anti-jargon** — Dentists are not SaaS buyers. No "enterprise synergy," no "omnichannel."
- **Warm but professional** — A trusted consultant who understands dental, not a cold software vendor.

Our Story

Software that fits how Indian clinics actually work

Most dental software was built for large Western practices — expensive, bloated, and disconnected from how patients here really communicate. Dentomate is built differently.

Why we built Dentomate

Running a dental clinic in India means juggling walk-ins, WhatsApp reminders, cash and UPI payments, lab work, and follow-ups — often on paper or across a dozen apps. Global tools cost more per month than many clinics earn from a full day of consultations, and they ignore the way Indian patients book, pay and stay in touch.

We started Dentomate to fix that: one affordable platform that handles patient records, treatment plans, billing, lab tracking and WhatsApp follow-ups — designed for Indian workflows from day one.

What we believe

  • Affordable, always. A free-forever plan that runs a real clinic, and paid tiers priced in rupees that make sense for Indian practices.
  • WhatsApp-native. Patients live on WhatsApp, so reminders and follow-ups do too — through the official Meta Business API.
  • Simple enough for the whole team. If your front desk can use it without training, we've done our job.
  • Your data is yours. Encrypted, hosted in India, and DPDP-aligned. See our Security and DPDP pages.

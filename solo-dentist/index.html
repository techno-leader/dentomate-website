Common Questions

What does dental software cost for a single dentist in India?

Dentomate is ₹0 on the Free plan, which covers up to 20 new patients a month. Starter removes that limit at ₹399/month or ₹3,990/year. Pro is ₹799/month or ₹7,990/year. Prices exclude GST.

Is Dentomate charged per user or per dentist?

No. Pricing is per clinic, not per seat. Adding a receptionist or an assistant doesn't change what you pay, so a solo dentist is never subsidising a seat-based licence model built for large groups.

What happens if I open a second clinic later?

Nothing is bought again and nothing is migrated. The Pro plan covers up to 3 clinics on the same login, and your existing patients, prescriptions and billing history stay exactly where they are. Recall campaigns and analytics work the same way at one clinic or three.

Do I need a server, an IT person or an annual maintenance contract?

No. Dentomate runs in a browser. There's nothing to install on a clinic PC, no local server to maintain, no AMC, and backups are handled for you.

Can I use it on a phone or tablet at the chair?

Yes. The interface works on a phone, tablet or desktop browser, so you can pull up a patient's history and allergies chairside without going back to the front desk.

Can I move my existing patient register into Dentomate?

Yes. Start recording new patients immediately and add older patients as they return. If you have a spreadsheet or an export from another system, contact us and we'll help you bring it in.

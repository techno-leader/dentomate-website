Trust & Security

Your patients' data, guarded end to end

Dentomate is built with security at the core — encrypted, access-controlled, backed up daily, and hosted in India. Here's exactly how we protect your clinic.

Encryption everywhere

All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2+ and encrypted at rest. Passwords are salted and hashed — never stored in plain text.

Role-based access

Every clinic controls who sees what. Admins, dentists and staff each get scoped permissions, so sensitive records are only visible to the right people.

Hosted in India

Application data is hosted on managed infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific region, aligned with India's DPDP Act. Read our DPDP page.

Automated backups

Your data is backed up automatically every day with point-in-time recovery, so an accidental deletion or outage never means lost patient records.

Isolated by clinic

Each clinic's data is logically isolated. One practice can never see another's patients, appointments or billing — multi-clinic accounts switch cleanly between locations.

Secure integrations

Payments run through PCI-compliant gateways (Razorpay) and messaging via the official Meta WhatsApp Business API — we never handle raw card data.

Security FAQ

Who can see my patients' data?

Only the users you invite to your clinic, each limited by their assigned role. Dentomate staff do not access clinic data except when you explicitly request support and grant permission.

Is my data stored in India?

Yes. Application data is hosted on managed infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific region, and we align our practices with India's DPDP Act, 2023.

What happens to my data if I stop using Dentomate?

You can export your data at any time. After account closure, associated personal data is deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period, subject to legal retention requirements.

Do you sell or share my data?

Never. We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the sub-processors required to run the service (such as WhatsApp messaging and payment gateways) or where required by law.

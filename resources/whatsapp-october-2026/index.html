WhatsApp Replies Will Cost Money from October 2026: What Indian Clinics Need to Do Now

Photo: StockyPics, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

Since November 2024, replying to a patient who messages you first has cost nothing on the WhatsApp Business Platform. That stops on 1 October 2026. Two specific things change, a free tier limits the impact for most small clinics, and there is one practical step to take before the end of September. This article covers all three.

What Changes on 1 October 2026

According to Meta's developer documentation, two message types that are currently free will become chargeable per message from 1 October 2026:

  • The free-form replies your receptionist or automated system sends inside a 24-hour customer service window. No template approval needed. Until October 2026 these cost nothing; from 1 October they are billed at the same per-message rate as utility templates. will be billed per message at the same rate as utility and authentication templates.
  • Utility templates sent inside an open The 24-hour period that opens when a patient messages your clinic first. Currently, replies and utility templates sent during this window cost nothing. From 1 October 2026 they are charged per message. will also be charged per message.

Until now, a clinic that replied to a patient's incoming message paid nothing for that exchange. From October, each of those replies carries a per-message charge, subject to the free tier described below.

What does not change: marketing templates (the recall messages you send to lapsed patients) were never in the free category. Those cost roughly ₹0.86 per delivered message and will continue at that rate.

The Free Tier: 1,000 Messages a Month

Meta is not removing the free tier entirely. As published on Meta's pricing page, each business phone number receives 1,000 free service messages per month. Meta only charges from the 1,001st service message delivered in that calendar month. The allowance resets monthly and does not roll over unused messages.

Utility templates sent inside a customer service window also draw from this same 1,000-message free pool, so the counter reflects all messages that were previously free, not just the free-form replies.

Meta does not offer volume tiers for service messages. Below 1,000 you pay nothing; above 1,000 every additional message is charged at the flat rate.

What a Small Clinic Actually Pays

The rate for service messages in India is the same as the current utility and authentication rate: approximately ₹0.115 per message, before the 18% GST that applies in India. Check Meta's published rate card for the figure that takes effect on October 1, which Meta committed to announcing by 1 September 2026.

Work out whether 1,000 messages a month is actually a binding limit for your practice. A single-dentist clinic seeing 20 patients a day and exchanging three messages per patient appointment would send roughly 1,200 service messages in a busy month. At ₹0.115 plus 18% GST, the 200 messages above the free threshold cost around ₹27. A two-dentist clinic with heavier inbound traffic might send 2,000-3,000 service messages a month, which works out to ₹115-230 before tax.

Those figures are not alarming, but they are new, and they need to be visible to whoever manages your practice expenses.

What to Do Before 30 September

There is one hard deadline: Meta has stated that any business phone number without a payment method on file by 30 September 2026 will have service message delivery stopped when charging begins on 1 October. That means patient replies stop working.

The action depends on how your clinic connects to WhatsApp:

  • Direct API integration: Log into Meta Business Manager, go to the billing section, and add a payment method. This takes about five minutes.
  • Through a A company Meta authorises to resell access to the WhatsApp Business Platform. Your messages travel through the BSP's infrastructure, and the BSP handles billing on your behalf. Examples include Gupshup, Wati, Interakt, and AiSensy. (BSP): Some BSPs handle Meta billing centrally and include the new service message charges in their platform fee; others require each client to add a payment method separately. Contact your BSP to confirm which arrangement you are on before 30 September.

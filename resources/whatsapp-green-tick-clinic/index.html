How to Get the Green Tick on WhatsApp for Your Clinic

Photo: StockyPics, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

You have seen a hospital or a dental chain on WhatsApp with a tick next to its name, and you want the same for your clinic. Agencies sell this as a "green tick package". Before you pay one, two things are worth knowing. The tick is not green any more. And there are two different ways to get one, depending on which WhatsApp your clinic uses.

This article goes through both, using Meta's own pages, so you can tell which one applies to you and what it really costs.

It Is a Blue Checkmark Now

Meta's Official Business Accounts page (last updated 19 September 2026) says these numbers show "a blue checkmark beside their name in the contacts view". The page does not mention a green tick anywhere. Agencies still use the old name because it is what clinic owners search for. When someone says "green tick", they mean this blue checkmark.

First, Which WhatsApp Is Your Clinic On?

A clinic runs WhatsApp in one of two ways, and each has its own route to a badge.

  • The WhatsApp Business app. The free app on the reception phone, where staff type replies by hand.
  • The Meta's paid service for sending WhatsApp messages from software instead of a phone. It is what people mean by "the WhatsApp API".. Messages go out from software, usually through a provider. You set it up by registering with Meta.

If you are not sure, you are probably on the app. A Platform account needs a Meta developer setup or a paid provider, and you would remember arranging one.

On the WhatsApp Business App: A Paid Subscription

The free app does not get the Official Business Account checkmark. Meta's page says it does not grant that status to "WhatsApp Business app phone numbers".

The app has its own badge, and it comes with a subscription. On 15 September 2026 Meta announced Meta One, a set of paid plans across Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp. Its business plans start with Essential, listed from $14.99 a month. Meta says Essential includes a verified badge, a verified channel on the WhatsApp Business app and protection against impersonation, all "pending successful verification". Meta also says plans, pricing and availability vary by region and by account, and that you see the options for your account when you start to subscribe. So the price in India is whatever the app shows you, not the dollar figure.

What this means in practice:

  • It is a monthly payment to Meta, not a one-time fee to an agency.
  • Meta checks the business before the badge appears. Paying is not the same as being approved.
  • You subscribe from inside Meta's own apps. Nobody else has to do it for you.

On the Platform: The Official Business Account

For a Platform number, the checkmark is the Official Business Account status on Meta's page above. Meta lists five conditions, and all of them must be true:

  • The business follows the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • It has been registered on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days.
  • Its business portfolio has passed Meta's check that the business account behind your number belongs to a real, registered business, using documents you upload..
  • The number has A PIN set on the WhatsApp number that is needed to register it again, so nobody else can take the number over. turned on.
  • Its display name has been approved.

The request itself is short. In WhatsApp Manager, open Overview and click the phone number, turn on two-step verification if it is off, then click Submit Request under Phone numbers, Profile, Official business account. Meta's page also describes an API call for the same request. Meta reviews it and sends a notification with the result. The page lists no fee for the request, and it gives no time for the review.

If Meta says no, you must wait 30 days before asking again. Meta's page says a refusal "does not limit your ability to share your business details": every Platform number has a business profile with a picture, email, website and description that you can edit at any time.

What the Checkmark Does Not Do

Neither of Meta's pages says a badge gets more messages delivered, more messages read, or more patients booked. Meta describes the Meta One badge as a way for people to know you are who you say you are, and the Official Business Account page describes where the checkmark appears. If someone selling a package promises higher delivery or open rates, ask where Meta says that. We could not find it.

Meta's own description is about identity and impersonation. So for a clinic, the badge matters most where a patient could mistake another account for yours, such as a clinic nearby with a similar name.

Before You Pay for a "Green Tick Package"

Ask the seller these, and get the answers in writing:

  • Is this the Meta One badge for my Business app number, or the Official Business Account checkmark for a Platform number?
  • If it is the Platform route, whose business portfolio will the number sit in, mine or yours?
  • Meta sets no review time. What happens to my money if Meta refuses?
  • What is your monthly fee after the checkmark, and is GST included?
  • Meta asks for at least 30 days on the Platform first. Are those 30 days included in what you are charging?

If moving the clinic onto the Platform is part of the package, read whether your clinic needs its own WhatsApp API first. For most small clinics the Platform account is the expensive part, and the checkmark is a request on top of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get the green tick on the free WhatsApp Business app?

Not the Official Business Account checkmark, which Meta does not grant to Business app numbers. The app has its own verified badge, which Meta includes in its paid Meta One business plans, subject to Meta verifying the business.

How long does the WhatsApp blue tick take?

For a Platform number, the business must have been on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days before it is eligible. Meta's page gives no time for the review itself. If the request is refused, you must wait 30 days to submit again.

Does the WhatsApp tick improve message delivery?

Meta's pages do not say so. They describe the badge as showing that a business is who it says it is. Be careful with any package that promises better delivery or open rates because of the badge.
